On arrival 10 min

  1. Walk the house once with the agent or seller. Lights on everywhere, blinds and curtains open, fans off, TVs off, toilet lids down, counters clear. A warm lamp in a daylit room: off.
  2. Fix what the prep list missed: cars out of the driveway (yours on the street, out of every window), bins out of sight, pet bowls, towels, chairs pushed in, beds squared, rugs squared or rolled, cords hidden, fridge bare, family photos and kids' names away, shower empty.
  3. Decide the station list; note anything skipped and why.

Per station 4 min · 6 frames + 1 clip

  1. Stage the frame: lights, blinds, chairs, cords, floor. Then the four edges: anything poking in moves, or pull back.
  2. Height from the legs, never the tilt. ~120 cm, keyed to the room: kitchen ~50 cm above the counter (under the upper cabinets), bath above the vanity into the basins, bedroom above mattress. Exteriors door/eye level.
  3. Level: roll and pitch to zero. More ceiling? Raise the tripod. Never tilt. Then yaw so the main horizontal (bed foot, island edge) is parallel to the frame bottom.
  4. C1, press once. Hands off until the fifth frame fires.
  5. Check the middle frame: 1/30 or slower indoors; faster is fine in daylight. ISO 125 and f/6.4 stay.
  6. C2, press once. Bounce up and behind you, never at the wall you're shooting. Mirrors: shoot from an angle; if the camera must show, a door or plain wall behind it.
  7. Video, one clip. Movie mode. Hold 3 s still · pan ¾ frame to the destination in ~3 s · hold 2 s · stop. (Slowed 2× in the edit: that is a 6 s pan.) No destination: hold 3 s, stop. Never across a bright window.
  8. The detail and the verticals. Hero rooms: 18-50 past 24 mm, C2 once (horizontal still), swap back. Then the phone, portrait, elbows in: two 4 s clips with one small move each. Other rooms with a feature: one phone clip. Stage the detail frame too.
  9. Anything moving (curtains, fans, pets, people): wait or turn it off. Motion between brackets ruins the fusion.

Gimbal pass 10 min · the moves between rooms only

  • Tripod clips carry the rooms; the gimbal only goes through doors, up stairs, out thresholds.
  • Normal walking pace, bent knees, heel-toe. Slow motion comes from the edit.
  • Every move starts and ends on 2 s of stillness. One clip each, in this order:
  1. Walk-up: start behind a post or hedge, still, walk to the door, still.
  2. Entry: one continuous move through the door into the living room; end on a feature or window, never a wall.
  3. Each hallway or stair: head-on, level. The neutral shot that lets the edit turn.
  4. Primary suite: push in from the door, then look back to the door or terrace (v1 missed it).
  5. Out to the grounds: dark interior to bright exterior, one take if you can; end outside, still, yard in frame (v1 ended at the door).
  6. Close: the only pull-back, from the best exterior angle.
  • Never tilt to hold verticals. Lower the camera.

Drone 15 min · after the gimbal, in daylight

  • Stills, one AEB each: A1 front ¾, roofline · A2 front straight on, 30–50 ft · A3 backyard · A4 the unique side · A5 context, 150–400 ft · A6 overhead (optional). A1–A3 minimum.
  • Video, 2 s still both ends, ~5–8 s move: V1 rising reveal · V2 half orbit · V3 push-in to the door · V4 pull-back and rise (closer).
  • Horizon on a third; front wall parallel to it. Heavy canopy, wind > 15 mph, rain, no LAANC: skip, note it in job.md.

The 18-50 when to swap · 4 times a house

  • Front exterior from the street (station 2), a full bracket station.
  • Front-door or porch detail at station 1 when the build quality shows; C2 once past 24 mm, files as st01_d1.
  • One detail still per hero room: kitchen range or island · living fireplace or view · primary headboard wall or window seat · primary bath vanity and tile.
  • Verticals are the social footage, on the phone: two per hero room, one in every other room with a feature, one of the facade. 10–12 a house, 4 s each, a tilt or a slide. A crop of a wide keeps a third of it.
  • Never for a room wide, never in a bare room.
  • Shoot them at the station, after the video clip, in station order; the tool matches phone clips to rooms by picture, not by clock. Zoom past 24 mm so the tool reads the still as a detail. C2 once, no bracket.
  • A detail is a material, a fixture, a view. Not furniture. Not the toaster.

Minimum shot list what the cut needs · tick at the door

  • Stills (bracket + pop): front exterior · living · kitchen · primary bed · primary bath; dining, other beds/baths, patio, yard one each where they exist.
  • Station clip, every station (v1 missed the patio): ¾-frame pan in ~2.5 s, then hold 3 s (or hold first). Big room: two or three pops, same position. Every light on. Hero rooms: three or four stills; the phone verticals are the video detail.
  • Gimbal first, in daylight, 10-18 at 10–12 mm, 2 s still both ends, ~4 s walks: walk-up · entry · one hallway or stair · primary look-back · out to the grounds · close (the pull-back).
  • Phone verticals, 4 s portrait, at the station after its clip: facade 1 · hero rooms 2 each · other rooms with a feature 1 each. Eight minimum, twelve the target.
  • Phone: Blackmagic Camera · 4K 60p · H.265 or ProRes · Apple Log (not HDR) · 1/125 · WB 5000 K · ISO auto. Grip out of frame. Fill job.md before the render.

Fast rules

  • Daylight, sun on the front. Book by the facade bearing: east AM, west PM; north = overcast or virtual twilight.
  • Height from the legs. Level both axes. Never tilt.
  • ISO 125 on brackets, 320 on the pop. Middle frame 1/30 or slower indoors; faster is fine in daylight.
  • One tripod position = one C1, one C2, one clip. Move on.
  • The tool fixes a degree of lean and the lens, not a tilted, moved or missing shot.
